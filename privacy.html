Game Night Gauntlet privacy

The short version: the app keeps what it needs to run your game night, nothing more. No ads, no tracking, no selling anything.

What stays on your phone

Your gauntlets, player names, game list, history and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. If you never turn on sharing or sign in, nothing leaves your phone.

What goes online

When you start a gauntlet, the app saves a live copy online so family can follow along with a code. That copy holds the gauntlet itself: player names, the games, scores and results. It is stored with a viewer code (6 characters) and a protected admin code. The admin code is stored only as a one-way fingerprint, so it cannot be read back.

Anyone who has a gauntlet's viewer code can see that gauntlet, so share it only with the people you want following along.

Email sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in, the app stores your email address and links your gauntlets to it, so you can find them again on another phone. We send you a one-time code by email, and the code is the only way to sign in. There is no password.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in: open Share & backup and choose Delete my account and data. That removes your email and every gauntlet linked to your account from the online copy.
  • Anything stored only on your phone can be cleared from the app's settings, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.
  • A shared gauntlet that is not linked to an account cannot yet be deleted from inside the app. Send the viewer code through the Ko-fi page below and it will be removed.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no tracking cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Services involved

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Online copies and sign-in are handled by Supabase. Sign-in emails are sent through Google's mail service. Tips go through Ko-fi, which has its own privacy policy, and the app never sees your payment details.
